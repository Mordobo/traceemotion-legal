Write to us

Have a question, is something not working as expected, or do you want to tell us what you think of the app? Write to support@mordobo.com, in English or Spanish.

To help you sooner, tell us what happened, your phone model and the app version (Settings › About › Version). You do not need to send us your journal or the exported file.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. TraceEmotion works without an account. What you write stays on the phone and, if you have it turned on, in the system's backup.

How do I export a copy?

Settings › Your data › "Export backup". The app prepares a JSON file and opens the phone's share sheet. The file is yours: it is not uploaded.

How do I delete what I wrote?

  • In the app: Settings › Your data › "Delete all". It asks you to confirm. It deletes moods, the journal, sessions and saved items. Your name and settings stay.
  • On Android, you can also uninstall the app or clear its storage in the system settings.
  • On iPhone, delete the app (touch and hold the icon › Delete App). "Offload App" can keep the data.
  • The backup is not deleted instantly by the button in the app. Manage it in your Google account or in Settings › your name › iCloud.
